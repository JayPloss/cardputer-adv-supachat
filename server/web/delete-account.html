Delete an account

The fastest option is Account → Safety & privacy → Request account deletion in the Android app. If you cannot sign in, submit the username or email associated with the account below.

We verify requests before deleting an account. Deletion covers the SupaChat account profile, room memberships, notification destinations, and user content where removal is legally and operationally appropriate. Safety records and restricted backup copies may be retained temporarily when required.

You may also email info@supachat.net.

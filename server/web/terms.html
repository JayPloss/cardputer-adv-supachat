Terms and community standards

By accepting these terms and using SupaChat, you agree to use its private rooms respectfully and only with an account you are authorized to use. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's account and use.

Keep people safe

Do not use SupaChat to threaten, harass, bully, exploit, impersonate, defraud, or invade another person's privacy. Do not post illegal content, hateful abuse, sexual exploitation, or content that encourages self-harm or violence.

Child safety: Child sexual abuse and exploitation are strictly prohibited. We act on reports, preserve or remove material when appropriate, and report confirmed illegal material to the relevant authority as required by law.

Invitations and rooms

Do not publish private invitations, passwords, or room content outside their intended audience. Room administrators may add or remove members and may remove content or accounts to protect members and the service.

Report and block

Use the message safety menu to report objectionable content or block a member. Reports go to SupaChat administrators for review. For urgent safety concerns, contact info@supachat.net. SupaChat is not an emergency service.

Service availability

SupaChat is provided as a small private service. Features may change and temporary interruptions may occur. Access may be limited or removed when necessary for safety, security, or violation of these standards.

Contact

Terms, moderation, and child-safety contact: info@supachat.net.

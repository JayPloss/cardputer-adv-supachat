Privacy policy

SupaChat is a private, invitation-only messaging service operated by Jay Ploss. This policy explains the information SupaChat uses to provide the service.

Information we handle

  • Account information supplied through Authentik, such as username, display name, email address, and account identifier.
  • Room membership, messages, voice notes, delivery/read receipts, presence, and safety reports.
  • Android notification tokens and basic device labels used to deliver message notifications.
  • Security and operational information, such as request times and network addresses available in server logs.

How we use it

We use this information only to authenticate members, deliver SupaChat features, administer private rooms, respond to safety concerns, secure the service, and honor account or data requests. SupaChat has no advertising and does not sell personal information.

Service providers

SupaChat uses privately managed server hosting, Authentik for identity, and Expo's notification service when an Android user enables notifications. These providers process only the information needed to perform those functions.

Retention and deletion

Account information and user content are retained while needed to operate the service. Safety records may be retained when reasonably necessary to protect users or meet legal obligations. Deletion requests are verified before account data is removed; residual copies may remain temporarily in restricted backups until those backups expire.

Request deletion from the Android app or at supachat.net/delete-account.

Security and choices

SupaChat uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Access is restricted by invitations, authentication, and room membership. No system is perfectly secure, so report suspected account misuse promptly.

Contact

Privacy, safety, and child-safety contact: info@supachat.net.
